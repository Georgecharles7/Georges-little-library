About George

George loves books and believes stories can inspire imagination, curiosity, and kindness. After losing all his books in the Eaton Fire, he remembered how it felt to lose something precious. That experience inspired him to start George’s Little Library so other kids who lost their books could still have stories to read. He wanted to give all kids access to books and put a smile on their faces.

His Story

George started this library to make sure no child goes without a book after losing all his books in the Eaton Fire. He remembered the feeling of losing something precious and wanted other kids who had lost their books to still have stories to enjoy.

“Stories like to travel—help them go!”

How It Works

The idea is simple: take a book you would enjoy reading, return it later or pass it on to a friend, and leave a book if you can.

George's goal is to help grow a community where stories are shared freely.

Join Our Community

Do you have books to donate? We welcome gently used children's books that can be shared with other readers in the community.
