About

George's Little Library is a small community book exchange created to share stories, inspire imagination, and nurture a lifelong love of reading.

Books are meant to be shared. By passing stories from one reader to another, we spark curiosity and build connections throughout the community.

His Story

George started this library after losing all his books in the Eaton Fire. He remembered the feeling of losing something precious and wanted other kids who had lost their books to still have stories to enjoy.

A lifelong reader, George created George’s Little Library to make sure no child goes without a book. Every book shared is a gift of hope, imagination, and comfort.

How It Works

📖 Take a Book – Find a story you’ll enjoy.

🔄 Return or Pass It Along – Share it with another reader.

📚 Leave a Book – Help the library grow.
