About George

George loves books and believes stories can inspire imagination, curiosity, and kindness. George's Little Library was created as a passion project to share books with other children and families in the community.

The idea is simple: take a book, read it, return it or pass it along, and leave a book if you can.

His Story

George started this library after losing all his books in the Eaton Fire. He remembered the feeling of losing something precious and wanted other kids who had lost their books to still have stories to enjoy.

A lifelong reader, George created George’s Little Library to make sure no child goes without a book. Every book shared is a gift of hope, imagination, and comfort.

How It Works

📖 Take a book you would enjoy reading.

🔄 Return it later or pass it on to a friend.

📚 Leave a book if you would like to share one.

Our goal is to help grow a community where stories and knowledge are shared freely.

Join Our Community

Do you have books to donate? We welcome gently used children's books that can be shared with other readers in the community.

Together we can inspire the next generation of readers.
